## Setup (D1 pilot)

- `experiments/e03/ddpm3d.py`: CT-conditional 3D DDPM, MONAI DiffusionModelUNet (channels 32-64-128-256, 2 res
  blocks, attention at the lowest level, 41.3M params), input [CT, x_t], epsilon MSE over the whole 96^3 patch,
  linear beta 1e-4..0.02 / 1000 steps, target x0 = density - 1, EMA 0.999, AdamW 1e-4 cosine, batch 2/GPU x 4.
  Same TRAIN cache, patch sampler and CT normalisation as E02 U5.
- Sampler: DDIM eta 0; each step a Gaussian-blended sliding window over the slab. Validated: with an oracle epsilon
  the sampler returns x0 exactly (test_e03.py, 5 and 25 steps). Resume validated in smoke 206022 (20 -> 40).
- Eval protocol (prespecified, bounded): per VAL subject 96 slices centred on the valid range, in-plane eval-mask bbox
  + 32 px; metrics on the central 64 slices; DDIM 25/50/100, 4 fixed-seed samples (seed 10*sid+k), each sample and the
  sample mean, spread, histogram W1, gradient ratio; U5 3D U-Nets (u5_s11, u5a_ref75k) on the identical input.
- Size limit: the 64-256 channel model OOMs at batch 2 on the 24 GB A5000s (node004); 32-256 at batch 2 = 14.4 GB.
  Batch 4 OOMs. So the pilot is smaller-batch than U5 (global 8 vs 16); not compute-matched.
- Env: added einops (MONAI attention dependency) to the shared .venv with uv pip.

## Runs

- 206023 d1_pilot (node004, 4x A5000, code e03-7d9ba63): 0.57 s/it, 80k steps ~12.6 h, then eval.

- 206023 eval (all 10 VAL, reports/e03/d1_pilot_eval_80000.json): D1 sample-mean slab MAE median 0.372 (0.22-0.51), same for DDIM 25/50/100 for DDIM 25/50/100 and every
  seed, vs U5 0.017-0.027 on the same slabs. Near-identical across steps and seeds = systematic, not sampling noise.
- 206091 diag.py (e03-425b47f, one VAL patch, EMA and raw, bf16 and fp32): one-step x0 recovery from x_t is good up
  to t~700 (x0 MAE 0.003 @t10, 0.017 @t500, 0.045 @t700) and poor near the end of the schedule (0.21 @t900,
  0.54 @t999). DDIM from pure noise gives a global negative offset (pred mean -0.25 vs true -0.04), MAE 0.20-0.25,
  with or without sliding window, bf16 or fp32, plain loop or MONAI. Diagnosis: linear-beta schedule (terminal
  alpha_bar ~4e-5) + epsilon prediction gives the model almost no x0 signal at high t; early steps set a wrong
  low-frequency level that later steps do not correct (cf. Lin et al. 2024, zero terminal SNR / v-prediction).
  Not a code bug; a design flaw of the D1 pilot configuration. D1 as configured FAILS (A08: U5 >> D1).
