## Core job 204996 stalled -> resumed as 205119 (2026-11-13)
- u2f_s22 died at step 26000 at 12:37 ("Pin memory thread exited unexpectedly"); the other 9 runs slowed from 0.45 to >3 s/it after ~6 h and logged nothing after 16:03.
- Not memory: cgroup usage 62 GB, no limit, no OOM. Loader workers were at 100% CPU, ~70% of it in the kernel, ~80k minor page faults/s each, each with ~89 GB of target memmaps mapped. Kernel NUMA balancing is on (numa_balancing=1; node004 has 1.5e12 hint faults); its cost grows with mapped size.
- Fix (68f680a): fresh memmaps per sample (no per-worker cache). Data are index-determined, so resumed runs see identical samples. Resumed from last.pt (<=1000 steps lost per run) at 0.40 s/it.
- U5 caches memmaps per worker too (48 GB cache); at 32.6k steps it is still at 0.39 s/it, data wait ~0. Watch it; apply the same fix if it degrades.
