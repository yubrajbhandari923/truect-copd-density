## Core job 204996 stalled -> resumed as 205119 (2026-11-13)
- u2f_s22 died at step 26000 at 12:37 ("Pin memory thread exited unexpectedly"); the other 9 runs slowed from 0.45 to >3 s/it after ~6 h and logged nothing after 16:03.
- Not memory: cgroup usage 62 GB, no limit, no OOM. Loader workers were at 100% CPU, ~70% of it in the kernel, ~80k minor page faults/s each, each with ~89 GB of target memmaps mapped. Kernel NUMA balancing is on (numa_balancing=1; node004 has 1.5e12 hint faults); its cost grows with mapped size.
- Fix (68f680a): fresh memmaps per sample (no per-worker cache). Data are index-determined, so resumed runs see identical samples. Resumed from last.pt (<=1000 steps lost per run) at 0.40 s/it.
- U5 caches memmaps per worker too (48 GB cache); at 32.6k steps it is still at 0.39 s/it, data wait ~0. Watch it; apply the same fix if it degrades.

- U5 3D, INTERIM at 60k of 150k: 0.0217 / 0.054 / 0.956 / 0.824 (pure-voxel MAE 0.0177, slightly worse than u3_s11's 0.0162).
Findings (paired, 95% bootstrap CI over subjects):
1. The fov loss region costs in-body accuracy, consistently and far beyond seed noise: material Dice -0.024 [-0.031,-0.020] 10/10 (U3, and the same in U2), density MAE +0.0022 (U3) / +0.0037 (U1). Outside-body output becomes clean (overlay). Cause not established (hypotheses: capacity spent on the table/outside region; skin-edge ambiguity). Decision: keep loss_region=body for model development; for full-volume export, mask outside the body with a CT-derived body mask (to be built when export is needed). The fov runs stay as a documented negative result.
2. Seeds agree tightly (sd of median ~0.0005 MAE, ~0.0005 Dice), so single-seed differences of the size below are meaningful.
3. Joint heads (U3) vs single: density -0.0012 [-0.0016,-0.0007] 9/10 vs U1; material equal to U2. Joint is free/slightly better.
4. Slice context helps a lot: U4 vs U3 (both fov loss) material +0.022 10/10, density -0.0025 10/10; fissure MAE -0.046, vessel -0.034, spine cortical -0.015.
5. 3D (U5, interim) beats u3_s11: material +0.0205 [+0.015,+0.026] 10/10, density -0.0016 8/10, boundary 0.054 vs 0.076; Dice fissure +0.34, spine cortical +0.31, ribs cortical +0.13, vessel +0.07. Confounded with architecture/capacity (DynUNet 31M, instance norm vs 2D U-Net 7.8M, BN), patch sampling and voxels seen. U5 body-loss output is noisy outside the body (as u3_s11).
